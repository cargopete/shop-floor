Write the PR summary for the current branch against its base, at most ten
lines:

- line 1: the PR title, plain and specific
- what changed and why
- how it was checked (the commands you ran and what they showed)
- anything left, risky or UNVERIFIED
- `Closes #N` (or `Part of #N` with what's done)

No AI attribution lines. Print it; don't open or edit the PR unless asked.
